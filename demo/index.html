03 / A FIVE-MINUTE WALKTHROUGH

One shipment, two clocks.

Use the small example first. The full dataset follows the same engine rules.

01

Start with what was known.

The 8°C reading was measured at 9 AM and received at 9:05. At 11 AM it is two hours old, with a five-minute arrival delay.

02

A retry adds no evidence.

Click Next delivery once. The same event and revision arrive again. The table shows the retry; the model’s feature digest and prediction stay the same.

03

A correction arrives later.

Click Next delivery again. The corrected value is 5°C, received at noon. Keep scoring at 11 AM: it cannot change the past. Choose Try noon to use the correction.

04

Memory has a limit.

With capacity two, deliver shipments B and C. A is evicted. Inspect A and read the incomplete-history reason. The engine returns its learned prior while A is absent.

05

Recover the same state.

Save a snapshot, advance, then restore. The server compares the saved and restored prediction bytes. Try an invalid reload: the prior model keeps serving.

06

Keep the conclusion modest.

The supplied data is synthetic. Negative labels assume reports are complete after the six-hour horizon plus 48 hours. The model’s score is not a deployment decision.
